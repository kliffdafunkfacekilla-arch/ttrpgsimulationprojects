# Chapter 1: The History Before the Shattering

## The Dawn of the Celestial Clockwork and the Genesis Dragons

![The Dawn Of The Celestial Clockwork](images/ch_01_sec_8_the_dawn_of_the_celestial_clockwork.png)

Before the reckoning of time, there was only the endless void and the primordial expanse. From this chaos, the Prime Divinity emerged. Slowly and deliberately, the creator began to forge the great Celestial Clockwork, shaping the primordial essence piece by piece to bring order to the infinite. To complete the grand design, the Prime Divinity made the ultimate sacrifice, offering its own existence to serve as the final, master gear.

As this last piece was laid into place, the Clockwork engaged. The great gears began to turn, and the first true ticking of time echoed across the void. As the immense mechanism spun up to speed—in a grand, blinding expansion of light and fundamental law—the Twelve Powers burst forth from the primordial chaos. They expanded infinitely outward, pushed ever forward by the crushing, heavy pressure of the void behind them.

When the Clockwork finally reached its perfect resting spin rate, the chaotic pressure of creation stabilized. As the raw energies of the Twelve Powers twisted, coalesced, and mixed, they gave birth to the Twelve Genesis Dragons—the majestic, living results of the Prime Divinity's design.

The Forging of the Mortal Plane Attuned perfectly to the pure energies of creation, the immense forms of the Genesis Dragons naturally reacted to one another's presence. They fell into a divine rhythm, their movements syncing with the eternal cycles of the turning Clockwork. As they traveled their celestial paths, their massive forms crossed and passed through each other. The resulting friction, reactions, and cohesion of their energies forged the firmament of the mortal plane.

Eventually, these Genesis Dragons became permanently bound to one another and the world they wove, autonomously spinning in their eternal, life-giving cycles to maintain the fabric of reality.

The First Gods and the Celestial Tapestry From the cosmic firmament left in the wake of the Dragons' paths, the First Gods began to coalesce, forming much like the stars and celestial bodies taking shape in the dark. They numbered twelve, born from the raw power of the proto-universe.

These twelve deities were beings of immense passion and power. Over aeons, they interacted—they loved, they fought, they created offspring, and they destroyed. Some perished in divine conflict, others were killed, and some isolated themselves in the deep cosmos. Through their breeding, expansion, and destruction, what began as twelve deities multiplied exponentially, their descendants becoming the countless stars and galaxies illuminating the night sky.

The Birth of the Fae As the surviving, aware gods gazed down from the cosmos, they took notice of the mortal plane below. Here, born from the continued interactions of the divine and the intricate mixing of the Twelve Powers, mortal life began to emerge. In a moment of absolute, flawless cosmic harmony, the Twelve Powers aligned perfectly upon the earth, giving birth to the world's first and most powerful mortal race: The Fae.

## The Fae Ascendancy and the Awakening

![II. The Fae Ascendancy and the Primeval Order](images/ch_01_sec_5_ii_the_fae_ascendancy_and_the_primeval_order.png)

**I. The Firstborn and the Divine Friendship** In the primordial era before the Shattering, the mortal plane was a flawless tapestry woven by the Genesis Dragons and maintained by the turning of the Celestial Mechanism. In this epoch of unparalleled peace, the world knew no chaos, and magic flowed in serene, predictable currents. This harmonious world was the cradle for the first and most powerful of all mortal races: The Fae. Existing as immortal, luminous beings, they possessed a glowing beauty and boundless connection to the living world. They did not wield power to subjugate; rather, they existed in perfect concert with it, acting as living conduits for the flawless design of creation.

The First Gods, who had coalesced in the cosmos from the residual power of the Genesis Dragons, looked upon the mortal plane with wonder. Recognizing the Fae as kindred spirits—immortal cousins born from the very same cosmic fabric—these deities descended to walk upon the earth. The gods frequently visited the earthly realms, sharing their divine presence and forging deep, enduring friendships with the Fae in an age of unbroken peace.

**II. The Twelve Primordial Biomes** 

![The Twelve Primordial Biomes](images/ch_01_sec_7_the_twelve_primordial_biomes.png)

Through the Fae's innate harmony with the Twelve Powers, the world blossomed into twelve stable, idealized ecosystems known as the Primordial Biomes. The Fae stewarded these paradises, acting as caretakers, memory-keepers, and diplomats of the natural order:

- **The Gravity Spires (Nexus):** A high-tension landscape where the "grip" of the world was perfectly calibrated. Towering, smooth obsidian pillars leaned toward one another, held in a state of perpetual magnetic balance. The Fae here managed the structural bonds of the world, preventing landslides and ensuring the seas and mountains stayed firmly in their assigned places.
- **The Unyielding Foundation (Mass):** A geologically dense region that provided the world’s physical weight and permanence. Featuring "Stone-Oaks" with lead-dense bark, it was a land where the ground never eroded. The Fae reinforced the density of matter, ensuring that islands stayed afloat and the continental crust remained thick and stable.
- **The Kinetic Plains (Motus):** A high-velocity, synchronized ecosystem defined by perfect timing. Trees here vibrated with kinetic energy, their leaves moving in a rapid, melodic shimmy that created a global heartbeat. The Fae regulated the speed of the world, controlling the velocity of the winds and the pace of the changing seasons.
- **The Shifting Mistlands (Flux):** A delta of transformation where matter existed in a state of pure potential. Within these "Mirror Groves," the trees and waters were semi-liquid, constantly shifting. The Fae oversaw the transformation of states, guiding the metamorphosis of creatures and turning rain to mist.
- **The Healing Cradle (Vita):** A hyper-vibrant, life-giving jungle that served as the world's primary nursery. The air was thick with glowing moss and medicinal pollens that instantly repaired any biological damage. The Fae acted as the world's master gardeners, ensuring organic growth was vigorous and free from disease.
- **The Eternal Sanctuary (Lex):** A silent, crystalline cathedral that embodied the unbending laws of physics. The trees were geometric, translucent, and completely immovable—they could not be burned, cut, or broken. The Fae here guarded the "Unbending Rule," ensuring the fundamental constants of reality never wavered.
- **The Silver-Leaf Archives (Ratio):** A living library that processed and stored the history of the world. Trees with silver bark possessed leaves that functioned like vellum pages, pulsing with arcane blue light as they recorded every event in the vicinity. The Fae acted as the world's memory, utilizing psychic energy to recall centuries of history.
- **The Orchards of Sacred Geometry (Ordo):** A landscape of absolute symmetry and mathematical reasoning. Every branch and stream followed perfect geometric angles, moving in a flawless dance. The Fae applied universal logic to the environment, preventing natural disasters by weaving perfect offsets to any imbalance.
- **The Prism Heights (Lux):** High-altitude cliffs that captured the full spectrum of celestial light. "Prism-Pines" refracted sunlight and starlight into thousands of colors, illuminating the biome even in the deepest night. The Fae were the masters of perception, guiding the sun and moon on their correct celestial paths.
- **The Premonition Willows (Omen):** A sensitive, whispering woodland that reacted to the shifting tapestry of fate. The leaves would change color or turn inward long before a storm or environmental shift occurred. The Fae acted as an early warning system, sensing when the great gears of the heavens were slipping and alerting their kin.
- **The Gilded Vales (Aura):** A tranquil glade that emitted an aura of profound emotional harmony. The biome induced a sense of peace so deep that conflict was physically impossible within its borders. The Fae used this sacred peace to act as the primary diplomats of the early age.
- **The Echoing Steppes (Anumis):** A spectral grassland where the spirit of existence was anchored. White trees cast no shadows, and the grass moved without wind, carrying the voices of the past. The Fae anchored the spirits of the departed here, ensuring their ancient wisdom remained as a persistent echo to guide the living.

### III. The Harmony of Life and the Awakening of Man 

![The Fae Ascendancy](images/ch_01_sec_9_the_fae_ascendancy.png)

Because the Fae existed as perfect, living conduits for the powers of creation, and because the visiting gods walked peacefully among them, the world brimmed with a miraculous abundance of wildlife. The harmony of the powers gave rise to wondrous, perfectly adapted flora and fauna that flourished under the loving stewardship of the Fae. The world was a self-sustaining ecosystem of unparalleled beauty.

It was from this vibrant, perfectly calibrated wilderness—born from the cohesive mixing of the Twelve Powers—that the crowning achievement of mortal life finally awoke: the twelve fledgling species of humanity.

### THE TWELVE ORIGINAL TRIBES OF MAN: THE UNTAINTED AWAKENING

![III. The Awakening and mentorship of Man](images/ch_02_sec_1_iii_the_awakening_and_mentorship_of_man.png)

**I. The Pure Lineages of the Clockwork** In the dawn of the Era of Order, the twelve fledgling species of humanity awoke within the twelve idealized Primordial Biomes . They were not the modern animal-kin who walk the earth today; they were the pure, untainted children of the Celestial Clockwork, born in pure human forms . Recognizing their own duty to the world's equilibrium, the immortal Fae acted as their selfless shepherds, teaching these early mortals the deep secrets of the earth—how to coax life from the soil, harness the gentle flow of rivers, and read the ancient language of the stars . Each tribe was perfectly attuned to the harmony of their specific homeland, existing in peace under the patient guidance of their mentors .

**II. The Twelve Tribes and Their Sacred Homelands**

- **The Tribe of Might (Mass):** Awakened in the Unyielding Foundation . Living among lead-dense Stone-Oaks, they were taught to respect the crushing pull of the earth and the physical weight that provided the world's permanence .
- **The Tribe of Endurance (Ordo):** Awakened in the Orchards of Sacred Geometry . They dwelled in a landscape of absolute symmetry, learning the principles of eternal stasis, unyielding structural integrity, and mathematical harmony from the Fae .
- **The Tribe of Reflex (Motus):** Awakened in the Kinetic Plains . They lived in a high-velocity ecosystem where leaves vibrated to create a global heartbeat, embodying kinetic grace and swift motion .
- **The Tribe of Finesse (Flux):** Nurtured in the Shifting Mistlands . Existing in a delta of pure potential among semi-liquid Mirror Groves, they were taught the fluid breath between forms and the seamless dissolution of boundaries .
- **The Tribe of Vitality (Vita):** Born in the Healing Cradle . They were the first mortal gardeners, dwelling in a hyper-vibrant jungle thick with glowing moss and medicinal pollens, living in perfect concert with the raw spark of biology .
- **The Tribe of Fortitude (Nexus):** Formed in the Gravity Spires . Walking among towering obsidian pillars held in perpetual magnetic balance, they learned the secrets of unbreakable atomic stability and the unyielding bonds of matter .
- **The Tribe of Logic (Ratio):** Awakened as the early stewards of the Silver-Leaf Archives . Surrounded by trees with silver bark that pulsed with arcane light, they studied the cold, perfect geometry and celestial constants of the grand equations .
- **The Tribe of Awareness (Lux):** Awakened in the Prism Heights . Dwelling on high-altitude cliffs where Prism-Pines refracted starlight, they learned from the Fae to command perception and bend celestial light .
- **The Tribe of Intuition (Omen):** Nurtured in the Premonition Willows . Inhabiting a sensitive woodland that reacted to the shifting tapestry of fate, they learned to read the subtle weave of destiny and predict the flow of the future .
- **The Tribe of Charm (Aura):** Raised in the Gilded Vales . They inhabited a tranquil glade emitting profound emotional harmony where conflict was physically impossible, mastering the spiritual frequency of all living things .
- **The Tribe of Willpower (Lex):** Awakened in the Eternal Sanctuary . They dwelled in a silent, crystalline cathedral among unbending trees, learning the absolute enforcement of cosmic law and divine command .
- **The Tribe of Knowledge (Anumis):** Born in the Echoing Steppes . Living in a spectral grassland where white trees cast no shadows, they anchored the spirits of the departed and learned the primordial blueprints and ancient lore of the universe .

### THE MENTORSHIP OF MAN AND THE SEEDS OF HUBRIS

### I. The Seeds of Discontent and Jealousy 

![The Growth of Greed and Jealousy](images/ch_02_sec_12_the_growth_of_greed_and_jealousy.png)

As the twelve species of man grew in number and strength, the harmonious tutelage of the early age began to fracture. Humanity 's quick life cycle and resaulting outlook on the universe and the passage of time, caused a deep mis understanding of how each other saw the world. The fae taught Humanity  how to commune with the world's power, but they did not think to teach them how to temper their own desires. The communal sharing championed by the Fae slowly withered, replaced by a fierce and aggressive individualism.

Man began to view the natural world not as a shared, living tapestry, but as a vast repository of wealth waiting to be claimed. Where the Fae had taught them to gently use a single stream to irrigate a field, ambitious men sought to divert two, then three, believing that greater consumption was always a virtue, regardless of the devastation it brought to the land or their kin. They fundamentally were in a hurry and more  always saved time later  and got more done in the same amount of time

Soon, the shadow of jealousy fell over the twelve tribes. The species, once united under the Fae, began to eye each other’s successes with envy. A tribe that had been taught the Fae's secret of refining metals hoarded that knowledge, leading to a burgeoning sense of superiority and the first subtle imbalances of power. This was the first true schism—a bitter competition for the Fae's favor and the hoarding of arcane truths.

### II. The Birth of Hubris 

![The Birth of Hubris](images/ch_02_sec_13_the_birth_of_hubris.png)

Greed soon gave way to the far greater sin of hubris. Humanity ceased to view the Fae as revered mentors, seeing them instead as rivals and living reminders of a dependency they wished to cast off. The turning point arrived when man began to master the fundamental powers of the world, arrogantly believing they could surpass their divine creators.

The ancient, cyclical magic of the Fae, rooted in natural ebb and flow, was dismissed by humanity as too slow and constrained. Instead, man developed crude, violent forms of sorcery—a dark magic rooted in brute force and rapid consumption. Seeking immediate gratification rather than long-term harmony, they accelerated growth and sought to conquer all mortal limits, completely disregarding the Fae's desperate warnings of causality and balance.

Claiming to be the ultimate culmination of the grand design, humanity began to mimic the Fae's divine authority. The twelve species constructed colossal cities designed to dwarf the most ancient Fae settlements, building grand monuments to their own vanity that mocked and destroyed the surrounding nature rather than honoring it. In their ultimate offense, they demanded that the Fae hand over the secret of true immortality—not as a humble request, but as the inherent right of their self-declared supremacy.

### III. The Fae's Sorrow and the Great Ward The immortal Fae watched this descent with increasing sorrow and profound alarm. Realizing that the knowledge they had imparted was being twisted into weapons of control and devastation, the Fae slowly began to retreat, sadly sealing away the deepest secrets of the world. Blinded by arrogance, humanity interpreted this withdrawal not as an act of protection, but as an act of fear.

Ancient Fae seers began to speak of a dark prophecy known as "The Shadow of the Magistars"—a looming future where humanity's hubris would congeal into a single, tyrannical authority that would bring untold suffering to the mortal plane. Knowing a dark age was imminent, the Fae took one final, desperate action to protect the world's most vital anchors. They wove a powerful, shimmering, sky-high magical boundary—a great ward meant to shield the Sacred Groves and their surrounding lands from humanity's aggressive, endless expansion.

### IV. The Ignition of the First Wars 

![IV. The Seeds of Hubris and the First Wars](images/ch_02_sec_2_iv_the_seeds_of_hubris_and_the_first_wars.png)

This ultimate act of protection became the catalyst for disaster. The magical ward did not unite the twelve species of man in shared reverence or fear; instead, it served only to enrage them, cementing their belief that the Fae were denying them their divine birthright.

Trapped by the barrier's limitation on their expansion, humanity's aggressive hunger for dominion immediately turned inward. The fragile alliances between the twelve species snapped under the weight of hoarding and jealousy. Realizing they needed all 12 powers to gain  the secrets of the fae and break the barriers they tried to gain each others knowledge of the power non trusting the others and instead of joining forces or working together in harmony they horded there secrets and sought to steal the others leading to the First Wars of Resource and Pride—a brutal, self-destructive era  where humanity turned on itself and turned the power into weapons of destruction.

The following generations saw stronger tribes wage campaigns of conquest to steal the unique arcane secrets the Fae had gifted to their weaker kin. While the weaker tribes used their knowledge to outsmart the bigger tribes, blood was shed and no one tribe gained any advantage or knowledge over another, and they kept performing larger acts of destruction. To fuel this sudden, violent growth, man developed crude sorceries rooted in brute force and the rapid consumption of the power and the life it created.

## The Magistars Dominion and the Exodus

### I. The Crag of Final Judgment and the Unified Dominion 

![V. The Magistars' Dominion and the Flesh-Lathes](images/ch_02_sec_3_v_the_magistars_dominion_and_the_flesh-lathes.png)

Out of the blood-soaked mayhem of perpetual war, twelve ruthless warlords and sorcerer-kings emerged as the undisputed masters of their respective peoples. Realizing that they sought to take the faes secrets but were only killing themselves and were  facing mutual annihilation over a torn land, these twelve assembled upon the a large crag before the central and grandest of sacred groves .The meeting was orchestrated by Kyros the Whisperer, who presented a cold ultimatum: unite to enslave the world together or killeach other in the shadow of the Fae’s barrier. Coerced by the logic of focused power, they swore the _Twelve Oaths of Iron and Blood_, using the power to bind them together  in an unbreakable blood ritual, pooled their stolen Fae-lore into a single vault, and declared themselves the supreme Magistars of the Unified Dominion.

### II. The Weaponization of the Planet Under the iron fist of the Magistars, the Dominion became an engine of tyrannical exploitation and resource consumption. The tribes skilled in irrigation were forced to divert entire river systems to the Magistars' colossal capital, while those who knew metalworking labored in brutal, subterranean mines to arm the endless legions. This was not a peaceful spread of culture but a relentless internal plunder; the Magistars constructed massive cities designed to function as power-collection arrays, utilizing destructive sorceries that caused ecological devastation to deliver immediate, immense power for their experiments.

### III. The Ritual of the Timeless Core and the Stasis Cycles The original Magistars realized that their mortal lifespans were too short to amass the power required to shatter the Fae’s barrier. To guide their centuries-long project personally, they twisted the Fae’s lessons on life and time into the _Ritual of the Timeless Core_. They constructed twelve great spires of precious metals to act as anchors for their souls and power.

A horrific cycle was established: while eleven Magistars were placed into deep stasis within the spires, one stayed awake to rule for an entire age. The waking tyrant maintained the massive blood sacrifice requirements and oversaw the growth of the Dominion's war machine until their own flesh grew weak. At that point, they would perform the ritual to transfer another Magistar into a specially bred host, placing themselves into stasis while their successor took the reigns of the world. They repeated this cycle for generations until their network to channel the power of the earth was complete.

### THE TWELVE LINEAGES OF MAN: A RECORD OF THE FLESH-LATHES

IV. The Age of Blood and Flesh (The Flesh-Lathes)

 During these long cycles of stasis and rule, the Magistars utilized constant sacrifice and magical manipulation to re-engineer humanity itself. In captive cities turned into breeding farms, the twelve pure human lineages were systematically broken and warped. Through cruel biomancy, the Magistars gave each tribe a burned-in affinity for a specific power, twisting them into specialized living weapons to serve as their masters' arsenal. They were mutated into bulging muscled hulks for siege work, immovable men of stone to hold the lines, and slender, multi-jointed, four-armed parodies of men to execute complex arcane tasks.

**I. The Twelve Warped Lineages** Today, the original human forms are entirely extinct. The twisted descendants of these twelve broken tribes are the horrific, immortal biological experiments and aberrations that remain locked in the dark ruins, while the true lineages of man have vanished from the earth.

- **1. The Kin of Mass (The Mass-Touched):** Originally the tribe of the Unyielding Foundation. Under the brutal command of Grom (Tiraton), they were bred to endure the crushing gravity of deep-core mining. They were twisted into "immovable men of stone".
- **2. The Kin of Ordo (The Ordo-Touched):** Originally from the Orchards of Sacred Geometry. Under Isolde (Stagus), they were warped to survive absolute zero and perpetual siege without requiring rest.
- **3. The Kin of Motus (The Motus-Touched):** Awakened in the Kinetic Plains. Under Anya (Vecelo), they were bred for impossible, light-speed violence and twitch-responses to serve as vanguard shock troops.
- **4. The Kin of Flux (The Flux-Touched):** Nurtured in the Shifting Mistlands. Under Leoric (Aurgenas), they were twisted for liquid grace, political infiltration, and the ability to slip through any boundary.
- **5. The Kin of Vita (The Vita-Touched):** Born in the Healing Cradle. Under Sorel (Gavusrix), they were subjected to cancerous biomancy, bred to survive plague, toxins, and the horrific environment of the biological laboratories.
- **6. The Kin of Nexus (The Nexus-Touched):** Formed in the Gravity Spires. Under Vahn (Carulkem), they were forged in thermal rage to withstand the absolute heat of the Magistars' weapon foundries.
- **7. The Kin of Ratio (The Ratio-Touched):** Originally the stewards of the Silver-Leaf Archives. Under Kyros (Metrion), their emotion was stripped, and their bodies were fractured into "slender multijointed 4 armed parody's of men" to execute perfect, unfeeling calculations.
- **8. The Kin of Lux (The Lux-Touched):** Awakened in the Prism Heights. Under Rictus (Opecten), they were warped to possess absolute, all-seeing vision and dominion over the skies to serve as the ultimate surveillance state.
- **9. The Kin of Omen (The Omen-Touched):** Nurtured in the Premonition Willows. Under Lyra (Termhill), they were twisted to navigate pitch darkness and sense the unseen future so they could hunt down hidden rebels.
- **10. The Kin of Aura (The Aura-Touched):** Raised in the Gilded Vales. Under Malaki (Virantor), they were bred to radiate intoxicating influence, serving as the beautiful, dominating wardens of the enslaved masses.
- **11. The Kin of Lex (The Lex-Touched):** From the Eternal Sanctuary. Under Zeila (Tyrustis), they were bred for rigid hierarchy, pack-dominance, and unwavering labor to serve as the unbreaking commanders of the slave armies.
- **12. The Kin of Anumis (The Anumis-Touched):** Born in the Echoing Steppes. Under Eldra (Lophex), they were mutated to hoard secrets, survive the slow march of time, and scurry unnoticed through the hidden cracks of the world to steal lore.

### V. The Bargain and the Prime Corruptor 

![VI. The Bargain, the Exodus, and the Weeping Wound](images/ch_02_sec_4_vi_the_bargain_the_exodus_and_the_weeping_wound.png)

After several cycles of stasis, the Magistars unleashed their full war machine against the Fae wards. The absorption of power by their colossal engines was so great that it began siphoning energy directly from the barriers, causing them to falter. As the Fae struggled to maintain their groves and some began to die from the strain, the Elder Fae offered a desperate truce: they would share their knowledge and provide advisors if the Magistars ceased their hostilities.

Among these Fae representatives was Sh'lar. Seduced by the speed and immediate results of human blood-magic, she became the Prime Corruptor. She betrayed her kin to teach the Magistars how to reverse-engineer Fae immortality and revealed the thirteenth, hidden force underlying reality: the School of Primordial Chaos. Empowered by this dark tutelage, the Magistars began capturing and sacrificing Fae by the hundreds, absorbing their essence to turn themselves into near-divine, localized hurricanes of destruction.

### VI. The Great Exodus and the Weeping Wound Realizing their very souls would only provide the Magistars with the tools for universal destruction, the Fae Elders made the heart-wrenching decision to abandon the mortal plane entirely. Twelve Elders voluntarily dissolved their physical forms in a monumental ritual of sacrifice, tearing open a massive portal  so their remaining kin could escape. This desperate exodus required power of such magnitude that it literally tore a crack in the Celestial Clockwork, leaving a "weeping wound" in reality—a cosmic weak spot that finally drew the attention of the gods.
