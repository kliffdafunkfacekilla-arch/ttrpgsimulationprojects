# Ostraka Setting Rules

## No Humans
When generating art, illustrations, or descriptive text for the Ostraka Setting (fantasy map generator / wiki), you must **NEVER** include humans. The setting strictly forbids humans.
- Instead of generic terms like "warriors", "riders", "merchants", or "people", explicitly use descriptors like "beast-folk", "fox-kin", "hound-humanoids", "insectoid-creatures", or other appropriate animal-folk variants depending on the specific chapter/lore context.
- Always include prompt constraints to actively avoid generating standard human characters in art.
