### The B.R.U.T.A.L. Engine: Character Creation & Progression Overview

##### 1\. Design Philosophy: The Lodestar of Ostraka

The B.R.U.T.A.L. Engine is a high-velocity, high-lethality framework designed to eliminate the arithmetic bottlenecks of traditional systems. We prioritize "low-friction" engagement—minimizing the Game Master’s mental load while maximizing the tactical torque of every action. This engine is calibrated for high emergence, where gear, biology, and the "3-Beat Staggered Refresh Economy" interact to create cinematic, brutal outcomes.**Systemic Foundations:**

* **Low Cognitive Load:**  We’ve stripped the chassis of spreadsheet-based bloat, removing math-heavy tracking in favor of immediate mechanical resolution.  
* **3-Beat Staggered Refresh Economy:**  Action timing follows a predictable rhythm, governing stamina, movement, and the "Taxation Protocol" for sustained effects.  
* **Tag-Driven Mechanics:**  Numerical modifiers are replaced by a robust Tag system (e.g.,  *Exposed* ,  *Dread* ,  *Impact* ), allowing for complex tactical interactions without the need for a calculator.  
* **Ouroboros Balance:**  Character depth is derived from the interaction of the twelve stats and elemental schools, where every strength creates an inherent systemic vulnerability.

##### 5\. The 12-Tier Progression Architecture: Template Overview

Progression is a vertical climb through 12 tiers, shifting from horizontal utility to extreme mechanical efficiency.**Track Functional Roles**  | Power Tracks | Survival Tracks | Talent Tracks | | :--- | :--- | :--- | |  **Physics Alteration:**  Generates battlefield tags, reactions, and zone-wide anomalies. |  **Resource Preservation:**  Mitigates damage tiers and permanently reduces Beat-taxes. |  **Tactical Lethality:**  Manipulates Advantage, creates openings, and coordinates the crew. |

##### 6\. Template 1: The Power Tracks (Sparkborn & Null)

1. **Affinity:**  Establishes the roleplay "spark" or "void" indication (e.g., Nexus fogs or Silicon ripples).  
2. **Minor Manifestation:**  Small, 1-Beat effect using elemental tags (e.g., small FLAME or PULL).  
3. **Simple Spell:**  Bread-and-butter 1-target effect using 1-2 tags (1-2 Beats).  
4. **Shield Manifestation:**  Passive resistance or clearing tags of the element following you on the Great Wheel.  
5. **New Spell Form:**  Unlocks shapes like Line, Cone, Burst, or Trap for Tier 3 effects.  
6. **Second Effect Type:**  Unlocks a secondary elemental verb (e.g., Mass adds "Lift" to "Crush").  
7. **Sustained Effects:**  Maintain an anomaly at a Focus cost of 1 Beat per round via Taxation.  
8. **Breaker Exploit:**  Offensively bypass armor of elements preceding you on the Great Wheel.  
9. **Zone-Wide Form:**  Power effects expand to fill one entire defined Zone.  
10. **Shapehold Without Beat Tax:**  Maintain one sustained Shape without paying the roundly Focus tax.  
11. **Constant Aura:**  Radiate a permanent passive power effect across the immediate vicinity.  
12. **Battlefield-Wide Form:**  Legend-tier effects affecting multiple Zones with massive chain reactions.

##### 7\. Template 2: The Survival Tracks (Action Verbs)

1. **Instinct:**  Defines the character’s survival affinity (e.g., smelling storms or sensing shelter).  
2. **Minor Defensive Manifestation:**  Low-impact trick like kicking up dust for COVER (1 Beat).  
3. **Basic Defensive Technique:**  Bread-and-butter move to reduce damage by 1 tier or resist hazards with Advantage.  
4. **Beat-Tax Reduction:**  Permanently reduce the Beat Tax by 1 on specific actions like Dodging or Resisting.  
5. **Defensive Variant:**  Apply Tier 3 techniques to allies, terrain, or as a triggered reaction.  
6. **Team-Defense Beat Trick:**  Allies near you reduce Move Tax or gain a \+1 Stamina refund when defending.  
7. **Major Defensive Technique:**  Signature move to intercept attacks or create temporary SAFE terrain.  
8. **Defensive Stance:**  1-round transformation granting Advantage on all defensive rolls and \+1 Stamina Beat.  
9. **Safe Zone:**  One entire Zone becomes STABLE/SAFE, negating environmental tags.  
10. **Defensive Rule-Bend:**  Permanent hack (e.g., immune to flanking or forced movement).  
11. **Team Shield:**  Once-per-encounter save reducing damage for the entire crew.  
12. **Guardian Moment:**  Legendary protection across 3 Zones, clearing all negative ally tags instantly.

##### 8\. Template 3: The Talent Tracks (Crew Roles)

1. **Style Affinity:**  Establishes roleplay edge (e.g., "always knowing who is lying").  
2. **Minor Offensive Trick:**  Tactical 1-Beat move like creating a brief OPENING or DISTRACTED tag.  
3. **Basic Support/Control:**  Bread-and-butter move to impose Disadvantage or MARK a target.  
4. **Advantage Boost:**  Permanent Advantage on rolls for Awareness, Tactics, Charm, or Initiative.  
5. **Offensive Variant:**  Apply Tier 3 techniques to multiple allies or entire Zones.  
6. **Team-Offense Beat Trick:**  Allies gain \+1 Move Beat or Advantage when attacking your MARKED target.  
7. **Major Offensive Technique:**  Force enemies into an EXPOSED state or create exploit-ready PRESSURE tags.  
8. **Offensive Stance:**  1-round rally; allies gain Advantage and you gain \+1 Move Beat.  
9. **Zone Pressure:**  Transform a Zone into a tactical killbox, imposing EXPOSED on all enemies within.  
10. **Offensive Rule-Bend:**  Break combat logic (e.g., ignore all cover or always act first).  
11. **Team Assault:**  Once-per-encounter strike granting the entire team Advantage for a round.  
12. **War Cry:**  Legendary rally granting Advantage to all allies and exposing all enemies in 3 Zones.

##### 9\. The Ouroboros Law: Combat & Elemental Relations

The engine's Aetheric torque is driven by the  **Great Wheel** , a sequence of twelve elements that dictate how power is "starved" or "fed."**The Sequence of the Wheel:**  Lithium (Will) → Osmium (Might) → Tungsten (Fortitude) → Gold (Finesse) → Silver (Vitality) → Lead (Reflex) → Iridium (Intuition) → Titanium (Endurance) → Silicon (Logic) → Bismuth (Knowledge) → Chromium (Awareness) → Phosphorus (Charm) → (Returns to Lithium).

* **Shield Manifestation (Tier 4):**  You gain passive resistance or the ability to effortlessly clear negative tags associated with the element that directly  **follows**  you on the Wheel.  
* **Breaker Exploit (Tier 8):**  Your attacks violently bypass armor or automatically apply vulnerable tags if the enemy relies on the element that directly  **precedes**  you on the Wheel.

##### 10\. Technical Glossary of Engine Terms

* **Beats:**  The fundamental unit of the engine's action economy. Actions, movement, and stamina are measured in Beats.  
* **Capacity:**  The mechanical reservoir for a character's internal power; it governs the ability to manifest anomalies and trigger high-output traits.  
* **Composure Damage:**  Psychological health reductions caused by social weaponry (Dread, Awe) or chaotic environmental feedback.  
* **Focus:**  The specific Beat currency required to maintain sustained effects via the Taxation Protocol.  
* **Tags:**  Non-numerical descriptors (e.g., FLAME, EXPOSED, IMPACT) that interact with tracks to define situational reality.  
* **Taxation Protocol:**  The mechanical system governing the round-by-round cost (usually Focus Beats) of maintaining sustained anomalies.  
* **Trauma Tiers:**  The system for tracking physical damage: Minor Injury, Major Injury, and Permanent/Lethal Damage.  
* **Zone:**  The spatial unit of the battlefield. Spells, movements, and defensive techniques typically target or protect an entire Zone.
